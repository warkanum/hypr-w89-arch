# Stargate scene prompts — second set

Status: all seven wallpapers generated on 2026-11-04 with the built-in imagegen tool.
Target: `assets/wallpapers/`, 2560×1440 PNG, 16:9.
See the [first set](STARGATE-SCENE-PROMPTS.md) and [collection ledger](WALLPAPERS.md).

Public episode transcripts supply the scene anchors, not verified production
drafts. Lighting, camera angles and environmental details are creative additions.
Each generation uses its scene prompt followed by the shared brief, prefixed with
`Use case: stylized-concept` and `Asset type: desktop wallpaper`.

Saved all seven files below as 2560×1440 PNGs. ImageMagick resized the
1672×941 originals to cover and centre-cropped to 16:9. Existing assets were
preserved. Visually reviewed all seven for composition and unwanted text.
The Resurgence drone is an artistic machine design with landing legs resting on
wreckage, rather than an exact model of the episode's drone.

## Shared generation brief

```text
Create one continuous wide 16:9 desktop wallpaper, 2560x1440 PNG. Cinematic realistic science-fiction concept art with physical materials, atmospheric depth and a clear focal silhouette. Keep the left third quiet and dark for desktop icons; place the principal subject toward the right. Retain subtle shadow detail, restrained highlights and natural texture. No people, readable text, numbers, logos, watermark, UI, collage or anime. Follow the scene-specific exclusions. Do not add unrelated objects or extra portals.
```

## 1. Crystal Skull — a bridge into nowhere

File: `stargate-crystal-skull-abyss.png`

Source: SG-1, [“Crystal Skull” transcript](https://imsdb.com/transcripts/Stargate-SG1-Crystal-Skull.html).
Anchor: a crystal skull reached by a bridge inside an enormous alien chamber.
Creative direction: distant architectural view, with mist and indirect green light.

```text
Inside the immense alien pyramid chamber from Stargate SG-1's Crystal Skull, a narrow stone bridge without railings stretches from the lower-right foreground to a tiny isolated platform suspended over a seemingly bottomless gulf. A human-sized transparent crystal skull rests on a low pedestal at the far end, small but visibly recognizable through a restrained glint. Vast stepped stone walls disappear upward into darkness; faint green light filters through mist far below the bridge. View obliquely from a shadowed side ledge to make the gulf and the fragility of the walkway unmistakable. Dark basalt, oxidized jade and cold quartz reflections. Monumental scale and archaeological mystery. No Stargate, giant skull sculpture, floating holograms or visible beings.
```

```text
A quiet junction deep within the Tok'ra's grown crystal tunnels from Stargate SG-1. The main tunnel curves out of sight behind a thick crystalline buttress, while the left foreground remains dark brown and charcoal. Low concealed illumination refracts softly through the walls without turning them into neon. Eye-level wide architectural composition, tactile mineral detail, secrecy and quiet ingenuity. Creative direction: empty exterior pier at the instant of a strike; no claim that
```

```text
On an exposed outer pier of Atlantis during the enormous ocean storm from Stargate Atlantis's The Storm, one white-blue lightning bolt strikes a slender lightning rod at the far-right edge of a raised industrial grounding station. Show a low perspective along the deserted rain-lashed walkway, with restrained Ancient architectural details and the distant central tower almost lost in sheets of rain. Dark ocean waves rear beyond the pier railings; wind sweeps water sideways across the metal decking. The strike briefly reveals the scale of the storm and reflects in narrow wet seams. Navy, charcoal and sharp silver-blue accents, with broad dark foreground on the left. No people, protective dome, Stargate, fireball or electrical text effects.
```

```text

Its silver-grey segmented spire resembles the central architecture of Atlantis, weathered and patched with moss. Most of its original city remains completely buried. In the right foreground, a small eroded ravine exposes just a few enormous geometric metal ribs beneath soil and tree roots, quietly revealing that the landscape hides a machine-built structure. Observe from a dark wooded ridge, with the tower on the right third and long layers of morning mist softening the valley. Muted fern green, slate silver and a restrained pale-gold sunrise. Ancient engineering reclaimed by time. No ocean, floating city, full exposed city or Stargate.
```
