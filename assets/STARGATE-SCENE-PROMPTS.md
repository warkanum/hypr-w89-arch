## Shared generation brief

Append this brief to each scene prompt:

```text
Create one continuous 16:9 desktop wallpaper, 2560x1440 PNG. Cinematic realistic science-fiction concept art, physically convincing materials, atmospheric depth, restrained bloom and readable silhouettes. Keep the left third quiet and dark for desktop icons; concentrate detail toward the right. Preserve detail in shadows. No people, readable text, logos, watermark, interface, collage or anime. Alien engravings may appear as indistinct surface texture. Where a Stargate appears, give it a coherent circular ring with a generous opening, nine chevron housings and believable architectural scale. Active portals are flat blue watery surfaces. Dormant gates have open centres showing the environment behind them.
```

```text

## 2. Nox — the city that was always there

File: `stargate-nox-city-reveal.png`

Source: SG-1, [“The Nox” transcript](https://stargate.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nox/Transcript).
Anchor: a floating city is revealed above the forest and later disappears.
Creative direction: catch an imagined intermediate moment of the reveal at dusk;
the shimmer and architectural details are artistic additions.

```text
The hidden floating city of the Nox from Stargate SG-1 emerges above an immense forest at dusk. Show the city mostly solid, with only its outermost edge fading into a subtle optical shimmer. Elegant distant terraces and slender towers hover above treetops, with no visible engines or exhaust. Frame the reveal through dark moss-covered branches; the forest occupies the lower half, the city the upper-right third. Fine mist layers separate the canopy from the suspended architecture. Forest green, smoky violet and a restrained pearl glow. Make the scene feel peaceful, secret and technologically incomprehensible. No Stargate, weaponry or giant energy dome.
```

```text

Place the obelisk on the right third and leave the left sky calm. Evoke a deliberate message from an intelligence far beyond understanding. No Stargate, inscriptions, spacecraft or explosion.
